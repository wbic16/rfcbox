# rfcbox-value — internal positioning for ietf.phext and RFC Box

This directory holds the Revenue division's internal value layer for ietf.phext (every IETF standard in one MOAT-ruled phext; 10,052 records, three audit legs PASS, build READY) and RFC Box, its first consumer: what the artifact is worth, to whom, on what rights basis, and in what offer shapes — drafts and analysis only, prepared under the standing publicity hold, with nothing here published, sent, announced, or sellable until Will decides otherwise. Read in this order: `positioning.md` first (the segments, ranked, and the rights analysis that says what a buyer would actually pay for, since the RFC texts themselves are free), then `offers.md` (four assessed offer shapes with cited market anchors, hypothesis prices, and the recommended first offer, marked as a recommendation for Will's decision); the companion access guide at `~/workspace/your_files/ietf-access.md` is the settled technical record both documents stand on and neither contradicts.
